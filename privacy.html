Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Fajrise ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Apple Sign-In: When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address.
  • Prayer location: Stored locally on your device to calculate accurate prayer times. We do not transmit your location to our servers.
  • Prayer settings: Calculation method, jurisprudence preference, and alarm preferences — stored locally on your device via SwiftData.
  • Account data: Your display name is stored in our database (hosted on Supabase) solely to identify your account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Fajrise app experience
  • To manage your account and process subscription-related actions
  • To respond to support requests

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not track your location in the background
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law

Subscriptions

Fajrise Pro is available as an in-app purchase managed entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings.

Data Storage

Account data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure. Prayer settings, location, and alarm data are stored locally on your device and are not synced to any server.

Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings tab in the app. Local data is also removed upon deletion.

Children's Privacy

Fajrise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
